The PanFS StorageClass operator is installed in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Next steps for a StorageClass:

1. Create a PanfsStorageClass in the namespace of the realm Secret. Its name becomes the name of the StorageClass.

   In the namespace of a TenantNetwork, nothing else is needed: the operator finds the TenantNetwork.

   apiVersion: panfs.vdura.com/v1alpha1
   kind: PanfsStorageClass
   metadata:
     name: <STORAGE_CLASS_NAME>
     namespace: <TENANT_NAMESPACE>

   In a namespace without a tenant network, name the Secret:

   spec:
     realmSecretName: <SECRET_NAME>

   The chart panfs-tenant-storageclass creates the same resource as a Helm release.

2. Check the result:

   kubectl get panfsstorageclasses -A
   kubectl -n <NAMESPACE> describe panfsstorageclass <STORAGE_CLASS_NAME>
   kubectl get storageclass <STORAGE_CLASS_NAME>
