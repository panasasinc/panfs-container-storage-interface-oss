The PanfsStorageClass {{ .Release.Name }} is created in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

The PanFS StorageClass operator now creates the StorageClass {{ .Release.Name }}.
Check the result:

   kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get panfsstorageclass {{ .Release.Name }}
   kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} describe panfsstorageclass {{ .Release.Name }}
   kubectl get storageclass {{ .Release.Name }}

The phase must become Ready. The phase Pending means that the TenantNetwork of the namespace is not ready yet.
A resource that stays without a status means that the operator is not running;
look at its pod in the namespace where the chart panfs-storageclass-operator is installed.
